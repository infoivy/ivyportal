# ISA Sales · Simple Discovery Framework

_By Abu Bilal · for phone setters._

## Opening

**Asalaam wa Alaykum, is this ___?**

_Wait for confirmation…_

Hey, this is **(NAME)** with the IVY Sales Academy team. I saw you filled out our form and I have a few minutes right now to go through it · sounds good?

Awesome.

**Set frame:** So basically I just want to see IF we can even help you out with regards to your goals, and if this is actually something you can find success in. If I feel like we can and there's alignment, I'll explain how we can do that step-by-step. Sounds good?

_Wait for confirmation._ Okay, great.

## Opening question

> So akhi, out of curiosity · when you saw our ad or video, what did you believe this could potentially help you out with?

**Typical surface-level responses (shows intent):**
- "I want to earn remotely"
- "I've been thinking about doing something new"
- "I want to do hijrah"

**Clarify the intent:**
- "Okay, makes sense · tell me more about that desire to earn remotely?"
- "Got it · what's making you want that right now?"
- "What's making you want to do hijrah right now?"

**Reasoning-level responses:**
- "I'm tired of the 9–5 and I want more freedom of time"
- "I'm trying to learn a skill I can implement in my business in the future"
- "There's a lot of fitna here and I'm becoming tired of it"

> ⚠️ **This is where most setters stop · you need to probe more!**

## Identify the pain

- "When you say you want more freedom of time · out of curiosity, what's been the biggest loss since working 9–5?"
- "Okay, makes sense · what do you feel has been the biggest challenge so far in regards to not having that skill right now?"
- "How long have you been feeling this way?"

**Personal-friction responses you'll hear:**
- "I'm unable to pray my salah due to my work hours and environment"
- "I've watched a lot of YouTube videos but I'm still lost · just spinning in the mud"
- "For the last 4 years… and recently I found out my kids were being taught about LGBTQ in school"

## Probe the impact

- "Subhan'Allah akhi, can I ask you a personal question? How does it make you feel that you're missing your salah?"
- "Hmm · why look into a more advanced training now specifically, as opposed to 3–4 months ago?"
- "Subhan'Allah akhi, out of curiosity · what do you feel can happen if you're unable to make hijrah and your daughter keeps getting exposed to that?"

> **This is where the real buy-in happens.** You'll hear things like: "I'm tired", "I'll feel like a failure to my family", "I don't want to waste any more time."

## Goals transition

- "How much will you need to be making a month to achieve [their goal]?" _(contextualize)_
- "And based on your current job, how much are you making at the moment?"
- "Subhan'Allah, you're only making [X] working that many hours? Out of curiosity akhi, how much have you been able to save with that salary?" _(get the exact amount)_

**Time:** "And akhi, if this allows you to achieve [goal] so you can [reason] · how much time are you able to commit to this daily?"

**Logistics:** "Out of curiosity akhi, are you familiar with Zoom and the basics of using a laptop?" _(ask for older leads)_

## The SET

> Based on everything you've shared · **[their goal]**, **[their situation]** · I genuinely think there's a real fit here, in shaa Allah. I want to get you on a call with **[CLOSER NAME]**, our senior advisor. On that call they'll walk you through exactly how you'll be able to [goal] if we were to work together. All we need from you is to join the video call from a quiet area. Does that work for you?

- "Okay khair · what timezone are you in?"
- **Ideally book them within a 72-hour window.**
- "Great · and just a reminder, [Senior Advisor] has a tight schedule, so be sure to be on time for the video call in a quiet area."

**After booking:**
1. Confirm the appointment time back to them clearly.
2. Send one relevant testimonial · someone in a similar situation who got results.

## Confirmation sequence

> Khair · you should have a confirmation email from **(closer full name)** with all the details: the time of your meeting and the joining link. Did that come through?

_(wait for them to confirm)_

> At the top or bottom of the email you should be able to accept the meeting invitation by clicking **Yes**, in shaa Allah · let me know once you've done that.

> I've also created a WhatsApp group chat to introduce you to **(closer name)** before your call · you should have received the invite link from me. Has that come through?

> I'm also going to send you some material to get you up to speed and on the same page as **(closer name)** for your call. Do you think you'll be able to watch that before your call and drop me a thumbs-up in the chat?

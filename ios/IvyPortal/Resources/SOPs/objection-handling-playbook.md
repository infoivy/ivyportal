Guarantees: 

    Let me ask you something - has anyone ever been able to guarantee success in life? Like, has anyone ever been able to promise that if you do X, you'll definitely get Y result?
    The truth is, the only person who can guarantee your results is you.
    
    Think about it - there are two types of people: those who need guarantees, and those who become the guarantee.
    
    The first person is always looking for someone else to take responsibility. "If you promise I'll succeed, I'll take action." But where does that mindset lead? To always waiting for permission, for certainty, for someone else to take the risk.
    
    Then there's the person who says, "I'm the guarantee." They understand something powerful - that their commitment, their determination, their refusal to quit is what actually creates results.
    
    So let me ask you this - if I could really guarantee everyone's success, wouldn't everyone be successful? Wouldn't everyone who went through this be crushing it? But that's not how the world works, is it?
    
    The people who succeed in this or anything else aren't the ones who got a guarantee - they're the ones who became the guarantee.
    
    So the real question isn't whether I can guarantee your success. It's whether you're willing to guarantee it yourself. Are you ready to be the person who commits fully rather than the one always looking for certainty?
    
    What are you gonna do?

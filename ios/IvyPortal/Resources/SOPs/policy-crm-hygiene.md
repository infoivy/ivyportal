# CRM Hygiene Policy

Close CRM is the source of truth for every conversation, booking, and outcome. Clean data = clean payouts, clean forecasts, and clean feedback loops.

v1.0 · Sales Team KPI

## Overview

Every lead we touch · inbound, outbound, referral · lives in Close CRM. This isn't optional record-keeping. Your CRM is your paycheck, your feedback loop, and your promotion case all in one place. This guide defines the standard, the timing, and what happens if the standard slips.

- **What to log**: Every conversation, every stage change, every objection · no phantom leads.
- **When to log it**: Within the same working session. Never 'I'll catch up on Sunday'.
- **Who owns it**: The setter who touched the lead. Handoffs are logged, not verbal.

## Policy Standards

Non-negotiables. If any of these aren't true at the end of your working day, you're non-compliant.

**01 · Every conversation logged same day**
If you had a DM, a call, or a story-reply exchange with a lead, it must be in Close before you sign off. No exceptions for 'busy days'.

**02 · Stage always reflects reality**
Lead statuses (New → Convo → Booked → Show → Close) must match the actual state of the conversation. A lead that went cold 3 weeks ago cannot still sit in 'Convo'.

**03 · Objection & constraint tags**
Every disqualified or delayed lead gets a reason tag: money / time / belief / deen / family. This drives our training and our follow-up SOPs.

**04 · Notes are searchable, not novels**
Notes use the format: [Objection] · [What they said in their words] · [Next action]. Screenshots and voice-notes are fine, but the structured line is required.

**05 · No duplicates, no phantom leads**
Search before you create. A lead you added but never actually spoke to is a phantom lead · delete it or mark it correctly. Phantom leads inflate metrics and are treated as data fraud.

**06 · Handoffs are written**
Passing a lead to a closer? Update the owner, add the handoff note, and tag the closer. 'I told him in Slack' is not a handoff.

## Consequence Structure

We believe in fairness and second chances. Consistent data hygiene, however, is non-negotiable.

> **The Grace Period**
> Each team member gets **two (2) instances** of non-compliance per calendar month without penalty. Use them wisely · they reset on the 1st.

**1st & 2nd Instance · Warning**
Formal Written Warning
Logged by your direct manager in your personnel file. Official notice to correct the behavior. No pay impact.

**3rd Instance · Action Required**
Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
You're placed on a 30-day formal PIP with weekly CRM audits. A pattern of non-compliance is now on record. Bonuses paused until PIP is cleared.

**4th+ Instance · Critical**
Disciplinary Action
Further action, which may include compensation adjustments, role change, or termination. Data fraud (phantom leads, backdated logs) skips straight to this tier.

## Implementation

How this actually runs day-to-day.

**Daily**

- Log every conversation before signing off
- Update lead stages as they change · not at end of week
- Submit your EOD with the same numbers Close shows

**Weekly (Sunday audit)**

- Manager runs a Close audit against your EODs
- Any discrepancy > 5% = an instance logged
- Team review of top objection tags in the Sunday call

**Monthly**

- Instance counter resets on the 1st
- Compliance score published on the team dashboard
- Top 3 cleanest CRMs get first pick on new inbound leads

**Escalation path**

- 1st–2nd instance → direct manager
- 3rd instance → head of sales + PIP
- 4th+ → head of sales + founder

> **Acknowledgement**
> By operating as a setter or closer on this team, you acknowledge this policy. Questions or edge cases go to your direct manager before the fact · not after.

# EOD & Meetings Policy

The EOD is the team's heartbeat. Every role files one, every single day · setters, closers, coaches, and CSMs. This page defines the standard, the timing, and what happens when it slips.

v1.0 · Every role · Zero-miss standard

## Overview

Everything this team measures · compliance, funnels, payouts, at-risk students, your own streak · is built from EODs. One missing report doesn't just hide your day; it corrupts the team's picture of reality. That's why the standard is absolute: **you do not miss an EOD. Ever.** The same applies to team meetings: if a meeting is on the calendar, you are in it.

- **Who files**: Everyone with a role: setters, closers, coaches, CSMs. No role is exempt, no seniority is exempt.
- **When**: Every day before 23:59 · seven days a week. There are no off days and no 'quiet days'. Zero is a valid answer.
- **Meetings**: Team meetings are mandatory, on time, prepared. Absence needs founder approval BEFORE the meeting · not an apology after.

## Policy Standards

Non-negotiables. If any of these aren't true at the end of your day, you're non-compliant.

**01 · One EOD, every day, before 23:59**
Seven days a week. Sick, travelling, slow day · the report still goes in, even if every number is zero. The form autosaves a draft, works on your phone, and takes under three minutes. 'I forgot' is a miss.

**02 · Zero is a valid answer · silence is not**
A day with no dials is a data point. A day with no report is a hole. Submitting honest zeros is fully compliant; not submitting is the only way to fail this policy.

**03 · Numbers match reality**
Your EOD must agree with the CRM, the calendar, and the revenue log. Dials you didn't make, sets that don't exist in the calendar, or cash that isn't in the deal log are treated as data fraud · not as a miss.

**04 · Same-day only · no backfilling**
EODs are written the day they happen, while it's fresh. Backdated reports filed days later don't restore compliance; the miss already happened and the data is already suspect.

**05 · Wins and blockers are real sentences**
One or two honest lines. 'Good day' is not a win; 'nothing' is not a blocker if you rebooked three no-shows. This is where coaching and fixes come from · write it like your manager reads it, because they do.

**06 · Team meetings: present, on time, prepared**
A calendar invite is a commitment. Arrive on time with your numbers already submitted. If you genuinely cannot attend, get founder approval before the meeting starts and catch up on the recording the same day.

## Consequence Structure

This policy is stricter than CRM hygiene on purpose: there is **no monthly grace allowance** for a silent miss. The only miss that doesn't count is one cleared with a founder *before* the deadline (genuine emergency, communicated in advance).

> **The Emergency Exception**
> Real life happens. If something serious prevents you from filing, message a founder **before 23:59**. An approved exception is not a miss. An unapproved miss explained the next morning is still a miss.

**1st Miss · Warning**
Formal Written Warning · same day
Your manager logs it and you hear about it the next morning, not next week. The expectation is that this is the only one you ever collect.

**2nd Miss (rolling 60 days) · Action Required**
Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
30-day formal PIP with daily check-ins on submission. Bonuses are paused until the PIP is cleared with a spotless streak.

**3rd Miss / Fabrication · Critical**
Disciplinary Action
Compensation adjustment, role change, or termination. Fabricated numbers or backdated reports skip straight to this tier regardless of prior history.

## Implementation

How this actually runs day-to-day · and how the portal makes it nearly impossible to miss by accident.

**Daily (you)**

- Submit on the EOD Reports page before 23:59 · phone works fine
- The amber 'EOD due' pill in the top bar means you haven't filed yet today
- The form autosaves a draft, so start it early and finish at night
- Protect your streak · it's on the page for a reason

**Daily (management)**

- Sales page shows who filed, who hit KPI, who is missing · live
- 'Missed yesterday' list is chased before 10:00 with the Copy-nudge button
- Every miss is logged as an instance the same day

**Weekly**

- Compliance and streaks reviewed in the team meeting
- EOD numbers spot-checked against CRM and revenue (see CRM Hygiene)
- Repeated Friday–Sunday softness gets called out · weekends count

**Escalation path**

- 1st miss → direct manager, same day
- 2nd miss → sales manager + PIP
- 3rd miss or fabrication → founders

> **Acknowledgement**
> By holding any role on this team, you acknowledge this policy. Edge cases go to a founder before the deadline · not after. The report takes three minutes; the trust it builds takes months. Don't trade one for the other.

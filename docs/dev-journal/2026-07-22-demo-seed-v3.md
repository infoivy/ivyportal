# Log: Demo seed v3 · realistic showcase data across every current surface

### Prompt
"add very realistic fake data, not the fake data set u used previously, for all facets ... fake csms, fake eods, fake students, fake action items, fake revenue ... fake setters and closers ... missed kpis ... fake check ins ... fake student requests as well"

### Issue
Demo data was fully removed 2026-07-13. The old v2 seed predates the entire new student model (Start Here locking, weekly EODs with per-call attendance, live tick table, two-mode daily logs, placements, pending-signups queue) and the founder wanted a fresh cast, not a rerun.

### What I did
Rewrote `scripts/seed-demo.mjs` (v3) and extended `scripts/remove-demo.mjs`. New cast: 4 setters (one deliberately struggling with missed KPIs and skipped days), 2 closers (with self-set deals for the 15% rule), 2 CSMs with $500 base pay, 1 coach, 3 role-less signups for the Requests queue, 1 linked student login. 14 students spanning the lifecycle with built-in story beats: interview inside 48h (Zubair), late installment + due-in-2-days (Tamim), stuck-in-Start-Here 4d (Ilyas), lazy 1:1 booker (Layth, 0 last week), ghosting + payment behind (Faris), two placed grads with testimonials (Rayan, Kareem), a scholarship student (Jibril). Data: 396 team EODs over 7 weeks, 215 two-mode student EODs, 9 weekly EODs with real call-name attendance arrays, 52 live attendance ticks, 27 1:1 calls with action_items_json, 21 adhoc action items, 11 placements, 13 CSM notes, 520 tallies, 14 deals ($50.2k upfront across two payout periods), 15 installment payments, 3 testimonials, 4 team-chat messages. Fictional-but-safe phone numbers (Ofcom +44 7700 900xxx / NANP 555 ranges). Ran the seed against prod.

### How I did it
Deterministic RNG (fixed seed), Riyadh-anchored dates so report_date matches the business day. Student emails are `@isa.demo` text; Ayaan's auth account is created BEFORE the students insert so the `link_student_record_by_email` trigger links + grants his student role automatically. Weekly rows compute `group_calls_attended` from the attendance array; last-week ticks are also mirrored into `student_call_attendance` for both weeks. Two PostgREST gotchas fixed mid-run: bulk inserts unify column sets (a scheduled call without `action_items_json` became NULL despite the column default → pass `[]` explicitly), and `student_placements.source` is check-constrained to `student|ivy`. Verified post-seed via founder-JWT SQL: 3 pending signups, 14 demo students (2 locked), 9 weeklies, 11 current-week ticks, 1 late payment, Ayaan linked.

### What was challenging
Teardown ordering: `student_weekly_eods.student_id` is ON DELETE RESTRICT, so remove-demo now deletes weekly/attendance/guide-step/placement rows before students. Realism vs safety: kept `@isa.demo` emails (undeliverable, cleanup-greppable) and reserved fictional phone ranges so nobody can accidentally contact a "student" while names/numbers read fully real. Demo alerts do reach the real CSMs' bell/queues — inherent to seeding prod; founder asked for it and removal is one command.

### How I did it
Deterministic RNG (fixed seed), Riyadh-anchored dates so report_date matches the business day. Student emails are `@isa.demo` text; Ayaan's auth account is created BEFORE the students insert so the `link_student_record_by_email` trigger links + grants his student role automatically. Weekly rows compute `group_calls_attended` from the attendance array; last-week ticks are also mirrored into `student_call_attendance` for both weeks. Two PostgREST gotchas fixed mid-run: bulk inserts unify column sets (a scheduled call without `action_items_json` became NULL despite the column default → pass `[]` explicitly), and `student_placements.source` is check-constrained to `student|ivy`. Verified post-seed via founder-JWT SQL: 3 pending signups, 14 demo students (2 locked), 9 weeklies, 11 current-week ticks, 1 late payment, Ayaan linked.
